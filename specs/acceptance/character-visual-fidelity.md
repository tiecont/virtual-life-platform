## Evidence and artistic review

| Class | Inspect at rest and in motion | Compare on matched footage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Approved silhouette, face, eyes/lashes, hair and costume, surface detail, socket contact and weapon. | Neutral/three-quarter portrait, eyelids and phonemes returning to rest, garment layers, wrist/elbow, foot plants, wing extremes, outline stability and action readability. |
| Pet | Approved profile and proportions, surface and material separation, articulation for its species. | Gameplay scale, rest/peak/return, seam stability and camera-facing readability during travel and attack. |
| Boss | Recognizable silhouette at encounter distance, part boundaries, scale and arena clearance. | Wind-up, hit and recovery from player view, elemental telegraph readability in motion, overdraw and frame pacing. |

Record the source hash, candidate revision, pose/time, camera transform, projection, illumination, resolution, hardware, objective measurement and linked frames for each defect. Record the independent reviewer's visual observation and recommendation separately. A critical defect breaks the approved identity, corrupts a source, or prevents playback. A major defect is visible at the target presentation distance or obstructs an action. A minor defect requires a close inspection and leaves the action legible. Reinspect adjacent frames and the return to rest after repair. The owner decides on artistic acceptance from the rendered material.

On a failed comparison retain the rejected outputs, revert the individual material, corrective track or derived mesh, and replay the exact capture. The preserved VRM and its existing scene remain the rollback source. Estimate further local work by its outstanding capture, rig or performance tests. Obtain a current provider quote and spending decision before any paid reconstruction.
