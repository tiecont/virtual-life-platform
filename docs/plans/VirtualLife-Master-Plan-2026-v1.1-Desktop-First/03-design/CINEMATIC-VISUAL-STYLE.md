# VirtualLife visual bible — bright anime-fantasy campus 3D

## Brand signature
- Mood = first-day-of-spring university town + cozy cafés + radiant fantasy magic + young social energy.
- Architectural grammar = pearl-white arches, blue slate rooflines, glass market awnings, sakura trees, mint courtyards, sunlit cobblestone and canal footbridges; strong street-level wayfinding and accessible entrances.
- Primary palette (from v1.0): `#F8FBFF` Mist White, `#EAF4FF` Cloud Blue, `#4B91FF` Aurora Blue, `#8168E9` Iris, `#F6A2C1` Sakura, `#69D7BA` Mint, `#F5CB74` Gold, `#1E3153` Ink Navy. Balance sky blue dominant + warm materials as contrast, not indiscriminate neon.
- Keep UI/UX physically grounded in environments: map terminals, café table placards, Pet Arena billboards, blue-gold teleport portals, readable signage and the `VirtualLife` wordmark.

## Dynamic scene visual hierarchy
- **Hero foreground**: expressive player avatar / one nearby NPC with detailed face, eyes, subtle ear/tail response, outfit fabric and skin/fur shading.
- **Midground**: café and market interior, ambient characters with simplified movement, soft shadows, animated foliage and banners.
- **Background**: academy, bridge, waterfall, distant airship and clouds; distant layers primarily baked/cheap dynamic rather than expensive full simulation.
- **Texture balance**: high detail clustered at faces/hands/quest objects, intentionally calm facades/sky avoid over-detailed “noise city”.

## Expression philosophy
Character emotions should read at 2m camera distance: body posture + eye shape + eyebrows + cheeks + ears + tail. Face capture is *not required*. Blink and eye gaze local; emotions published as semantic emote ID/tick, not per-frame full facial morph packet. Maid café SFW/staff interactions must remain respectful.

## Quality checks
Golden lighting noon, sunset, after-rain blue hour; café interior card table at readable camera; pet arena burst effect with no inaccessible flashing. Reduced-motion disables screen shake, excessive bloom, flickering and dangerous rapid flashes. Avatars must sit with tail/hand collision solved. QA results use actual Unity builds and reviewed screenshots, not AI concept alone.

## Intellectual property policy
Genshin/ZZZ/Star Rail are **quality references only**. All design language, character silhouettes, models, UI, soundtrack, shaders and names must be independent with per-asset rights and original concept approval. Logo mark requires trademark clearance before commercial release.
