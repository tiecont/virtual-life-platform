# Resources and timeline — scope controlled, no unsupported release dates

A visually polished anime city requires technical art/character animation/lighting/UX as well as systems and agent coding. Hardware, Unity licensing, signed distribution, art/music rights, iOS/macOS requirements and cloud infra **cannot be inferred** from the archive. Owners must price these at G0 and reserve people/agents accordingly.

## Suggested workstreams
1. Rust backend/DB/security/runtime; separate from launcher Rust code.
2. Unity native Windows engineering: render, input/camera, netcode, content/animation/UI.
3. Character/world artists and technical art: two anthro variants, Aurora Plaza and café, Pet Arena stage, original shaders/lookdev.
4. Next.js official website/news/support/download/auth.
5. QA/SRE: real PC GPU/driver matrix, installer signing, artifacts/CDN, server stress and rollback.
6. Gameplay design and human playtesting of café/table/Pet Arena loops.

## Scope-reduction order under budget pressure
First cut extra districts, extra Pets/species/outfit variants and advanced weather; keep movement/café/seat/card/Pet PvE/teleport, clear controls, Rust authority, readable animation, safe release, 30/60fps profile gate. No made-up deadline and no unreviewed launch promise. Plan scheduling after repo SHA and staffing audit.
