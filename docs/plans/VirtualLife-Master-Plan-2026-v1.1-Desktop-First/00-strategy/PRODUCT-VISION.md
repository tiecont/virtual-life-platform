# VirtualLife — Product vision 2026 desktop-first

**Pitch:** enter a bright fantasy-anime city as your own expressive anthro companion, explore on foot, navigate to a friend/café/Pet Arena, sit at a real game table, play and leave again — seamlessly in the same social world. VirtualLife is not an MMO promise of infinite districts on day one; quality of interactions is the product advantage.

## Player pillars
1. **Live in Aurora City**: third-person walking/running, optional click-to-move, readable POI map `M`, safe travel and friend invites.
2. **Be yourself**: kemonomimi and anthro avatar builder, warm face expressions, eye contact, ear/tail mood and coherent motion, original silhouettes/clothing.
3. **Places feel real**: café/maid café/arcade/card lounge/Pet Arena have entrances, actual tables, chair anchors, crowd ambience and game activities; no menu-only teleports pretending to be physical social spaces.
4. **Great games inside the city**: cards with hidden-hand privacy, Pet Arena x match-3/skills/roaming, game balance via Rust authoritative reducer/receipt; social participation woven into place.
5. **Polished, stable native game**: Unity URP client on Windows with measured 30/60 FPS tiers and art approval; optional cross-platform later.
6. **Welcoming website**: VirtualLife official site showcases trailer, world, download, news, account and support with high usability and zero requirement to run 3D open world.
7. **Fair/safe community**: no pay-to-win game stats, consentful SFW venues, report/block, privacy, moderation, crash/error recovery and transparent rewards.

## Initial vertical slice
A sunlit **Aurora Plaza**, café, card table, Pet Arena gate, one interactive NPC schedule, two fox/cat anthro variants of one rig, 12–18 facial targets, one Pet combat/tutorial and one safe teleport. Player can roam freely and interact, all accepted commands run on Rust server. Only two actual polished venue activities are necessary for proof; the world should *feel alive* through animation, lighting, sound and visual-only NPCs.

## Not included in first release
Photo-real long fur physics, seamless continent, expensive global illumination on every tier, VR, open creator economy, multiplayer battle royale, 1000 avatars visible, universal mobile day-one, full browser-client parity. Build scope and quality gates from evidence.
