The executable report whitelists only these finite aggregates and explicit unknowns. It does not spread raw telemetry,case data,trace IDs,participant identifiers or credentials into the handoff. Negative checks reject promotion of a historical sample to live monitoring and conversion of unknown cost/retention into zero.

Before any authorized launch,assign the roles in the private operator register,configure a private authenticated collector and tested alert recipient,review retention/access and rehearse signal outage. Keep operator endpoints behind the private network;public edge404 is required even with a valid token. Confirm Prometheus/query validation and actual delivery rather than assuming a template is deployed. All those public provisions remain outside this offline task.

During an authorized watch,record UTC window,artifact/version IDs,finite counter deltas,error classes,admitted versus held occupancy,DB resource observations and any approved action. Proposed sustained thresholds are in [resource budgets](RESOURCE-BUDGETS.md). Missing coverage,rights,readiness or privacy controls overrides healthy latency and keeps scope closed. Use [incident procedures](INCIDENTS.md);never auto-scale,raise quotas,purchase resources,send external messages or restore consent from a monitoring signal.

The final local status report is [offline handoff](../agent/OFFLINE-HANDOFF.md). PUBLIC_RELEASE remains false. A prepared watch and truthful report satisfy the local preparation task;they do not claim an operated post-release service.
