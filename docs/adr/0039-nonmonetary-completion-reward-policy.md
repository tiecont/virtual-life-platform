# ADR0039: Bounded nonmonetary completion reward policy

Status: Local policy model. Date:2026-11-03.

Audit found two original avatar outfits (casual,cafe-apron), durable focus completion and a transactional hospitality completion outbox, but no progression ledger, entitlement store or reward endpoint. M13-01 adds strict policy/observation schemas and a pure server decision function. It does not add ledger persistence, register consumers or grant XP. Existing focus time records prove timer completion, not work quality; progression never claims otherwise.

Default progression is disabled. Explicit trusted configuration may permit2XP for a validated focus completion in Deadline/Library, or2XP for a validated hospitality completion in Maid/Restaurant/Lounge. Each world is capped10XP/UTC completion day; account total is capped20XP/day across worlds. Park/dating have no rewarded completion kind and zero daily budget. Messages, friends, invites, online duration, dates/matches, romantic interaction, tips and moderator actions are not source types. No relationship score, paid attention, public leaderboard, money, trading or cash-out exists.

Hospitality also caps two completions with the same other participant per UTC day and rejects self-partner rewards. At least60seconds must separate granted completions; late observations are bounded24hours and cannot change the original completion day. Caps are checked for the whole grant rather than partially minting remaining budget. Current eligibility is mandatory. Source IDs and completion instants must come from a trusted engine adapter; no client endpoint accepts a reward observation, amount or usage counters. Replay uniqueness/account locks and ledger-derived usage remain mandatory M13-02 work. Pure policy arithmetic is not an exactly-once or collusion-detection claim.

The initial cosmetic policy uses existing original assets only: casual is a free baseline; cafe-apron unlock threshold starts at10 lifetime XP. XP is not spent/traded. Unlock does not grant staff authority or guarantee role-restricted appearance; M13-03 must independently validate role/shift/world and provide casual fallback. Starter appearance cannot depend on participating in optional social service. Consent withdrawal, block, report or refusal must never deduct XP or pressure someone to continue contact. No cosmetic rarity/loot box mechanism exists.

Tests cover disabled/unsupported worlds and sources, cap/cooldown/partner boundaries, current eligibility, clock rollback/late delivery/day mismatch and strict forged-input rejection. A TypeScript union-inference build failure was corrected with explicit permitted-kind typing, without relaxing schemas. Evidence m13-policy-checks.json reports actual checks. No DB migration, app composition, legacy mutation, production policy activation or media licensing change. Later ledger/consumer/equip/UI/rollback tasks remain unverified until implemented.
