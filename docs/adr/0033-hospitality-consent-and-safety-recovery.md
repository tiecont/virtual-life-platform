# ADR0033: Hospitality consent and safety recovery

Status: Accepted local implementation. Date: 2026-11-03.

Assigned staff may refuse unserved work without current room admission. POST orders/:id/refuse requires own assignment, expectedVersion and a durable idempotency key. The same transaction records a private refusal, requeues the existing order, clears unfinished preparation and increments its version. Refusing staff cannot see or reclaim that order, including after unblock. The response contains only a refusal acknowledgment; exact retries after reassignment reveal no peer state. Own export includes bounded refusal metadata; staff erasure removes it without deleting the customer's pending order.

Customers may withdraw PENDING/ASSIGNED/PREPARING/READY orders outside the room or after closure. POST orders/:id/withdraw cancels without completion or rewards. Sorted participant account locks, room-owner serialization and row/version checks serialize withdrawal/refusal with service and retries. Receipt failure rolls back refusal and requeue. Served service remains privately acknowledgeable; refusal cannot erase a course already served.

The social contact-withdrawal callback checks a real bilateral block and requeues assigned pre-serve work in the block transaction. Unfriend alone does not refuse service. A callback error rolls back both block and reassignment. Current block and shared SanctionPolicy admission also guard service commands, so no new contact waits for maintenance. Staff sanctions end the shift and requeue outstanding work; customer restrictions cancel unserved work when observed.

Recovery fairly observes twenty active orders per batch using the existing SQL recovery sequence. It cancels unserved orders after original customer visit loss/restart, final disconnect grace expiry, seat release, invalid attached sessions, safety restriction or a two-hour maximum age. It clears assignee/deadline and increments version without emitting completion. A staff requeue followed by stranded-customer cancellation can increment version twice. This deliberately supersedes earlier restart tests expecting PENDING work tied to an impossible old customer visit. Connected additional tabs or reconnect within fifteen seconds retain authority. Served/completed orders are excluded from abandoned-unserved cleanup.

The existing three-second maintenance loop and bounded batches provide observation, not a realtime cancellation SLA. Database outages propagate; they are not classified as permission denial. Report/block/moderation browser regressions were rerun, but no new hospitality-specific report UI or configured report context is claimed. No automatic report, sanction, progression grant, romantic roleplay or paid attention exists. Trusted injected ports remain review boundaries.

Migration0018 adds refusal records with account/order cascades, the restricted shift reason, mutation operations and fair recovery ordering. Rollback disables hospitality adapters while retaining data; no destructive down migration or legacy DB mutation. Evidence m11-safety-checks.json preserves initial failures, changed obsolete restart expectations and final actual verification. All real rollout flags remain OFF; actual Maid adapter remains M16.
