# ADR 0110 — Retain owner serialization after the diagnostic profile

Date: 2026-11-06. Status: accepted for N05-03; local correctness results are recorded separately from capacity qualification.

Retain the existing global owner queue, four-worker viewer projection, per-viewer reads and snapshot-carrying room.delta. Introduce no mailbox, projection cache, sparse delta or quota increase in this slice. The measured queue contention is real. This decision does not declare the queue fast enough for production or close the contention issue.

N05-02 and two fresh N05-03 runs use the same12-actor/two-room sandbox workload, hardware, Node/PostgreSQL versions and unchanged application source. Successful movement p95 varies264.16/234.31/253.20ms; mean owner queue wait varies128.75/114.24/129.26ms. Mean projection wall time is15.65/13.10/15.11ms per movement. The selected pathfinding cost is small; nested DB span sums overlap and cannot identify a CPU bottleneck. The repeated runs do not isolate a particular SQL read, cache strategy or serialization change as the cause of improvement. Delivered snapshots also vary294/286/294 despite identical attempted commands and zero unsupported/dropped packets. Scheduling around reconnect notifications is a possible contributor; its precise cause was not isolated. This is measured variability, not a bandwidth saving.

These short synchronized loopback bursts identify projection and cross-room queue contention as investigation targets. They omit mixed gameplay/business work, sustained occupancy, WAN/device constraints and representative privacy changes. The proposed50ms queue objective is not met by this burst; no SLO or sustainable capacity is approved. A mailbox would introduce per-account travel coordination, deterministic cross-room lock ordering and safety barriers. A cache would require invalidation across block, consent, session, world and appearance changes. Neither risk is justified by a controlled candidate comparison yet. Preserve the current security and transactional ordering while N08 develops qualified workload evidence. Do not change the measured target or mark it passed to justify retention.

The next optimization experiment should first isolate repeated projection reads with a mixed, source-pinned workload. Compare the same profile/hardware/source variant, report accepted and rejected outcomes separately and include cached-recipient invalidation while a block/revoke is pending. A real delta must preserve join/resync snapshots, epoch/version gap recovery and slow-consumer behavior. If a mailbox is later justified, prove account-level travel coordination and lock ordering, partition/owner failure and privacy barriers before replacing global serialization. Existing block/revoke and world-generation checks cannot be bypassed to reduce timings.

An offline [comparison tool](../../scripts/compare-runtime-profiles.mjs) validates completed cleanup, workload/budgets, environment, source identity and observed command outcomes. It reports differences with DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY qualification, never an optimization/capacity verdict. Its negative checks cover changed hardware/workload, unexpected rejects/timeouts, unsupported DB coverage, dropped/unknown delivery, invalid source and warming metrics. It has no runtime effect or credentials. Evidence/source manifests and correctness results are in [the comparison report](../architecture/N05-03-PROFILE-DECISION.json).

Rollback: remove the comparison tooling/documentation independently; no application data, migration, ownership or protocol rollback is needed. A future optimization must provide its own revert to this verified global-owner path and preserve all prior evidence.
