## Shared and World-Specific Behavior

Shared: authorized seated customer placement,private relevant-participant order projection,per-world policy/version,durable state/version,idempotent mutation receipts,atomic unique assignee,bounded queue/active work,voluntary shift checks,requeue with stale-version invalidation,one completion effect,transactional safety/contact denial and data lifecycle. Default reference path:PENDING->ASSIGNED->PREPARING->READY->SERVED->COMPLETED;customer cancellation before assignment;handoff returns unserved work toPENDING with incremented version. A world may restrict transitions but cannot skip authority or terminal guards.

Maid-specific: Customer/Maid naming,maid cosmetics/poses,ingredient minigame,recipe challenge and interaction points,NPC fallback presentation. Restaurant multi-course and Lounge text-only mocktails need their own MenuPolicy/WorkflowStrategy,not duplicated order tables. A player selecting a voluntary service role is never a safety moderator. Customer cannot choose assignee or compel service;staff can refuse/end subject to safe requeue. No romance/tip-for-attention/paid service is introduced.
