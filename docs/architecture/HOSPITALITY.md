## Implemented Core

POST `/v1/orders` requires an authenticated connected membership, owned seat, exact owner epoch/room/membership, trusted item and policy version. One account has at most one nonterminal customer order across worlds. Trusted policy bounds room queue and active staff work. Clients cannot choose customer, assignee, role, phase, timestamps, price or reward.

POST `/v1/orders/:id/claim` atomically moves PENDING to ASSIGNED with expectedVersion. POST `/v1/orders/:id/cancel` allows only the customer of a PENDING order. Both require current membership in the origin room. M11-04 adds prepare/ready/serve and M11-05 adds private completion/outbox below. Actual progression grants remain M13.

Staff authorization uses durable voluntary shifts only when the trusted policy explicitly offers staffing; otherwise it denies. An injected internal port remains for trusted test/adapter composition,not HTTP privilege input. Role labels and avatars grant no authority. Service shifts are separate from safety moderator privileges.

The room owner serializes live authorization/capacity with room changes, captures world generation before awaited authorization and checks owner/session/membership/generation before commit. SQL transactions reauthenticate under sorted identity account locks, lock order rows, validate versions/phases and persist changes plus mutation receipts together. A failed receipt insert rolls back the order. Twenty concurrent actual TCP claims yield exactly one assignment/version increment/claim receipt. This is single-owner PostgreSQL behavior, not distributed fencing.

Mutations require Origin, CSRF and UUID Idempotency-Key. Fingerprints reject changed input under the same key. Exact place retries observe the existing private result after restart without creating membership or orders. New mutations require live source; historical receipts cannot authorize new visits. New mutations30/minute/account and placements12/hour/account are durable limits; exact retries reuse receipts. HTTP/session/IP bounds also apply.

## Voluntary Shifts And Recovery - M11-03

POST `/v1/staff/shifts` requires strict source/policyVersion and Idempotency-Key. Only explicitly offered voluntary staffing admits a shift;one open shift/account and per-room staff cap are enforced. Duration is server-set four hours. GET `/v1/staff/shift` privately reconciles and returns one's latest shift or null. POST `/v1/staff/shifts/:id/end` uses strict schemaVersion-only body,Origin/CSRF/key and own account authorization;no live room is needed to withdraw. It atomically clears pre-serve assignments,requeues existing orders with increased versions and persists the receipt. New terminal-end keys are still charged against24commands/hour. Exact retries do not reactivate old shifts or end a newer shift.

Shifts bind epoch/room/membership/policy and require a connected current visit for staff commands. A remaining tab preserves authority. Last-tab disconnect interrupts service;the original assignment survives the15second room grace. Timely reconnect resumes the same shift. Expiry,missing visit,restart or grace timeout ends/requeues with version invalidation. Actual TCP/socket tests cover20staff competition using real voluntary shifts,not injected grants. Old-room context after restart is not a new admission;M11-06 cancels stranded unserved customer work.

Fair recovery observes at most20shifts/batch under the room owner and account/row locks,using a SQL sequence rather than clock equality. main.ts schedules it every3seconds and relevant endpoints also reconcile. Batching/DB outages can delay observation;15seconds is an earliest timeout,not a guaranteed realtime SLA. Shutdown awaits in-flight work. Served/completed work is not requeued;completion semantics are in M11-05 below. ADR0030 records the limits.

## Menus And Service Actions - M11-04

Trusted policy optionally supplies a strict MenuPolicy:unique items matching itemKeys,unique resolved workflow keys and OrderWorkflowStrategy timed-courses. Each strategy has1..5 unique courses with server preparation1..300seconds. POST `/v1/orders/menu` requires an authorized current source and returns labels/coursecounts/policyVersion only. Orders snapshot the chosen server workflow at placement;clients never provide workflow/recipe/duration/score. No price/payment or inventory exists. Legacy core orders without a workflow retain core operations but cannot run service steps.

POST `/v1/orders/:id/prepare|ready|serve` requires assigned,current on-shift staff,source/version/key,matching current policy,unblocked customer in the original current visit with owned seat and attached valid session. Prepare sets trusted readyAt;ready rejects before deadline;serve inserts a unique order/course ledger and increments state/version with the mutation receipt. All final effects rollback when receipt persistence fails. Concurrent same-key serve retries produce one course row. Intermediate course returns same order to ASSIGNED;last course reaches SERVED,not COMPLETED. No automatic timer completion/tick writes or progression reward exists.

Requeue clears unfinished readyAt and keeps the completed course index/ledger. Replacement staff restarts only unfinished preparation;old assignee receipts cannot run steps. Backward trusted clock denies state changes. Restart preserves workflow snapshot and course meaning but invalidates live membership/shift,so no new room admission is inferred. Migration0016 adds workflow,course_index,ready_at and engine_hospitality_courses with unique order/course keys. Account deletion/terminal retention cascades ledger rows. ## Completion And Transactional Progress Hooks - M11-05

POST `/v1/orders/:id/complete` is customer-only with strict schemaVersion/expectedVersion,Origin/CSRF/key. All workflow courses must have durable served receipts. It needs no current room to privately acknowledge past service. Staff/caller reward,time,event fields reject. One transaction changes COMPLETED/version and inserts a unique order completion,outbox link and mutation receipt. Outbox or receipt failure leaves the order SERVED. Terminal new keys observe state without new outcomes/receipts;exact old key with changed input conflicts. No client-controlled grant exists.

Internal OrderCompleted events are built from durable order/completion and contain scoped account/order/world/item/course/time metadata. No HTTP/socket/export/log exposes this internal payload. HospitalityOutbox supports at most8trusted stable-key consumers and20events/consumer/batch. It locks participant accounts/outbox row,rechecks a unique delivery receipt and commits hook SQL plus delivery together. Hook or delivery-receipt failure rolls both back;concurrent dispatchers/restart cannot duplicate committed effects. Sanitized retry metadata records bounded attempts/nextAt with1..60second backoff and permits other consumers to continue. A retry is not a delivery claim.

Consumers must be trusted DB-only callbacks on the supplied transaction:await queries,no network/detached tasks/transaction control. Exactly-once is limited to transactional DB effects. Noncooperative code remains a review gate;no user can register hooks. Default app has zero sinks and grants no progression;M13 owns actual handlers. main3smaintenance schedules drain and shutdown awaits it. Migration0017 creates completion/outbox/delivery/retry tables,all cascaded by order erasure/90day terminal retention. Future consumer-owned effects require their own privacy lifecycle;events are not an infinite archive or production SLA. ADR0032 details scope/rollback.

## Consent And Safety Recovery - M11-06

POST `/v1/orders/:id/refuse|withdraw` uses strict schemaVersion/expectedVersion with Origin/CSRF/key. Assigned staff may refuse unserved work without live-room admission. An atomic private refusal/requeue/receipt excludes that staff from this order's queue and claim even after unblock. Response is only schemaVersion/refused; exact retry reveals no reassigned customer state. Own exports add at most100 orderId/createdAt refusal entries with truncation. Customer withdrawal cancels unserved work outside room/closure without completion or reward. Neither endpoint can undo served courses.

Bilateral block requeues pre-serve assignments in the same social transaction, preserving served courses and clearing unfinished readiness; failure rolls back both effects. Unfriend alone does not refuse service. Every service command still checks current block/shared safety. Maintenance ends restricted/invalid-session staff shifts and requeues their work. Fair20order batches cancel unserved work for original customer visit loss/restart, grace expiry, released seat, no valid attached session, safety restriction or two-hour age. Served/completed service is excluded. Restart may first requeue staff work then cancel stranded customer work, incrementing version twice. Exact placement retry cannot reactivate a cancelled order.

Migration0018 adds private refusal cascades, recovery ordering and mutation/reason vocabulary. Three-second scheduling/batching/outages bound observation, not an instantaneous SLA. No automatic report/sanction/reward is created. Existing report/block/moderation browser flows pass; a dedicated hospitality report UI/context and real adapters remain future integration. See ADR0033 and m11-safety-checks.json. All six local M11 tasks are verified at shared-engine scope, not real Maid integration or public release.
