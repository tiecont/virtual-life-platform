# Grand Restaurant

M18-01 supplies an independently authored local world at `/worlds/restaurant`, using the shared room authority, identity, original Pixi scene and native keyboard controls. The package depends only on contracts and SDK. Runtime activation requires both nonproduction `LOCAL_RESTAURANT_WORLD=1` and an explicit registry flag; defaults and real rollout remain OFF.

Garden dining and Window dining each contain four reachable exclusive chairs. These are two areas of one public room, not private rooms. Eight dining seats fit within ten room places; the additional unseated places do not reserve staff capacity or grant authority. Table resource keys in the event policy match this inventory exactly. Admission and reads remain separate; opening the page/catalog never creates a room or booking.

Diner/Server/Cook perspectives use native radio controls. Choosing a perspective issues no staff/order mutation. The separate voluntary work panel starts or switches actual policy-bound Server/Cook shifts through the Hospitality owner. Restaurant world1.1.0/scene1.0.0 offers at most2workers per actual room,one open shift/account. Server may claim/serve;Cook may claim/prepare/ready. Wrong/off-shift actions are rejected on the server. Roles grant no appearance/moderator privileges;aprons remain forbidden. This staffing slice offers no menu or customer order controls yet. Existing assignment ownership remains enforced;multi-person handoff,menu/courses/coordination are M18-04. Dinner/private-table policy and full acceptance remain M18-05/06.

Role switch requeues unfinished assigned orders and clears preparation deadlines in the same transaction as persisted role/revision/receipt. It retains the original shift ID/start/four-hour deadline. Duplicate requests return canonical current state;receipt failure rolls the change back. Independent Stop voluntary work remains available outside room/world,while status reads fail or a role request is pending. Account revisions prevent older pending start/switch input from restoring work after stop. Last-checked own status expires after30seconds;network deadlines are8seconds. Existing disconnect grace,session/safety recovery,shift expiry/restart and90day retention remain shared-owner behavior. Natural recovery ending a role invalidates pending consent too.

M18-02 consumes Events table schedules/holds/confirmation/private own bookings/cancellation/holder check-in. These are virtual world-scoped table resources across room instances; a booking does not allocate a room/chair. Authorized organizers must schedule real slots through existing owner controls; installation does not invent schedules or provision an operator. Empty, failed and expired reads have distinct UI states. Exact uncertain retries retain their original key/body; snapshots expire in30seconds.

After personal check-in, the holder may explicitly publish a group in their current room. Each diner independently joins from an authenticated connected visit; partySize alone never counts peers. Anonymous current attendance counts are shared with visitors in that room for at most5seconds; small groups may infer who joined. This is explicit group disclosure separate from global profile visibility. No roster, peer account IDs, focus data or analytics consent is involved. SQL serialization/uniqueness enforce capacity and one current party/account. Invalid sessions, safety restrictions, bilateral blocks and stale visit/epoch cannot grant attendance. Offline members are reconciled on observation; reconnect may require a fresh voluntary join. Private dining policy remains M18-05.

Stop works independently of room/catalog/world and pending writes. A durable withdrawal revision prevents an old slow open/join from restoring consent. Own booking cancellation is available before check-in; CHECKED_IN remains history and its table resource stays allocated until slot end or organizer cancellation. Stopping participation withdraws group consent without rewriting attendance history. Event end/cancel closes participation independently of that history. Account/event deletion cascades; existing90day event retention and bounded20projections/64member histories per reservation/100own export apply. Historical IN records denote consent, not proof of current presence. No physical food sales, service completion, reward or staff grant is claimed.

Existing shared report/block controls appear during an actual visit. Release requires all social safety and operator gates; this local scene is not production approval. No external media, books, food photographs, audio or shipping data are included. Source provenance is served at `/assets/restaurant/provenance.json` and releaseApproved remains false.

Room capacity, seats, version checks, disconnect recovery and revocation use existing owners. Scene slice01 adds no persistence. Slice02 adds reservation-owned migration0026 for consent/memberships. Slice03 adds hospitality-owned0027 shift role_key/defaultstaff/accountrevision, preserving generic staff behavior and existing booking APIs. No alternate session or world database. See ADR0055/0056/0057 and `docs/agent/evidence/m18-scene-checks.json`, `m18-booking-checks.json`, `m18-roles-checks.json` for actual verification and limits. Disable the local world for rollback and retain additive data; destructive removal requires review. Strict older readers must upgrade before using role-bearing world responses.
