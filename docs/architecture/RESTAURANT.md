# Grand Restaurant

M18-01 supplies an independently authored local world at `/worlds/restaurant`, using the shared room authority, identity, original Pixi scene and native keyboard controls. The package depends only on contracts and SDK. Runtime activation requires both nonproduction `LOCAL_RESTAURANT_WORLD=1` and an explicit registry flag; defaults and real rollout remain OFF.

Garden dining and Window dining each contain four reachable exclusive chairs. These are two areas of one public room, not private rooms. Eight dining seats fit within ten room places; the additional unseated places do not reserve staff capacity or grant authority. Table resource keys in the event policy match this inventory exactly. Admission and reads remain separate; opening the page/catalog never creates a room or booking.

Diner/Server/Cook perspectives use native radio controls. Choosing a perspective issues no staff/order mutation. The separate voluntary work panel starts or switches actual policy-bound Server/Cook shifts through the Hospitality owner. Restaurant world1.2.0/scene1.0.0 offers at most2workers per actual room,one open shift/account. Server may claim/serve;Cook may claim/prepare/ready. Wrong/off-shift actions are rejected on the server. Roles grant no appearance/moderator privileges;aprons remain forbidden. Private dinner policy is locally verified in M18-05;full acceptance remains M18-06.

Role switch requeues unfinished assigned orders and clears preparation deadlines in the same transaction as persisted role/revision/receipt. It retains the original shift ID/start/four-hour deadline. Duplicate requests return canonical current state;receipt failure rolls the change back. Independent Stop voluntary work remains available outside room/world,while status reads fail or a role request is pending. Account revisions prevent older pending start/switch input from restoring work after stop. Last-checked own status expires after30seconds;network deadlines are8seconds. Existing disconnect grace,session/safety recovery,shift expiry/restart and90day retention remain shared-owner behavior. Natural recovery ending a role invalidates pending consent too.

M18-04 adds the original Garden dinner:starter/main/dessert,each prepared on a two-second server deadline. Service policy1.1.0 freezes a course-match kitchen-pass rule with the order. A seated diner orders;Cook claims/prepares/calls ready;Server selects the matching kitchen course,takes the plate and serves. This is two service workers plus one diner. Wrong matches,stale versions,unprepared plates,wrong roles or withdrawn/current-visit consent cannot transfer/serve a course. Pickup and receipt commit atomically;it does not mark food served. Intermediate service returns the next course to the kitchen queue;served-course ledger survives requeue and restart. Final service waits for the customer's separate acknowledgment and uses the existing unique completion/outbox transaction. No score,speed contest,tip,payment or new reward sink exists.

Native menu/private-order/queue/coordination controls have exact-key uncertain retries,8second deadlines,30second menu/own-history snapshots and10second queue observations. Refresh after another worker acts;the UI makes no automatic realtime claim. Withdraw unfinished service can supersede a pending UI request,while server versions serialize the actual outcome. Get own orders is bounded20/truncated and shows no peer identities. Current authorized staff see anonymous same-room tickets,never a diner roster. Former workers can lose access after transfer/requeue;their old receipt cannot reacquire ownership. The customer-owned history remains canonical. Generic workflows without handoff retain prior assignment transitions.

M18-02 consumes Events table schedules/holds/confirmation/private own bookings/cancellation/holder check-in. These are virtual world-scoped table resources across room instances; a booking does not allocate a room/chair. Authorized organizers must schedule real slots through existing owner controls; installation does not invent schedules or provision an operator. Empty, failed and expired reads have distinct UI states. Exact uncertain retries retain their original key/body; snapshots expire in30seconds.

After personal check-in, the holder may explicitly publish a group in their current room. Each diner independently joins from an authenticated connected visit; partySize alone never counts peers. Anonymous current attendance counts are shared with visitors in that room for at most5seconds; small groups may infer who joined. This is explicit group disclosure separate from global profile visibility. No roster, peer account IDs, focus data or analytics consent is involved. SQL serialization/uniqueness enforce capacity and one current party/account. Invalid sessions, safety restrictions, bilateral blocks and stale visit/epoch cannot grant attendance. Offline members are reconciled on observation; reconnect may require a fresh voluntary join. Invite-only audience restrictions are described below;they do not create isolated rooms or chairs.

Stop works independently of room/catalog/world and pending writes. A durable withdrawal revision prevents an old slow open/join from restoring consent. Own booking cancellation is available before check-in; CHECKED_IN remains history and its table resource stays allocated until slot end or organizer cancellation. Stopping participation withdraws group consent without rewriting attendance history. Event end/cancel closes participation independently of that history. Account/event deletion cascades; existing90day event retention and bounded20projections/64member histories per reservation/100own export apply. Historical IN records denote consent, not proof of current presence. Party commands perform no physical food sale,service completion,reward or staff grant.

M18-05 adds explicit room-public versus invite-only party audiences. An audience change withdraws existing guests and offers before publishing. The holder may offer a private dinner only to a current friend with invitation opt-in. A recipient accepts, separately reviews travel to the public room and then individually joins the party. Acceptance allocates nothing. Invite-only limits party membership and five-second aggregate counts;shared room presence,physical chairs and the listed calendar slot remain public. Uninvited party views and event discovery contain no private party IDs,counts or roster. Inbox metadata requires a current connected eligible holder;historical receipts never grant current access.

Dinner offers last at most120seconds;accepted eligibility ends at check-in close. Decline/cancel,block/unfriend,opt-out,Stop,audience changes and closure revoke consent;restart/stale visits/session/safety prevent current access. Inbox observations expire after30seconds and refresh manually. Native offer/accept/decline/withdraw,exact-key retry,explicit travel review,private counterparty Block and confidential contact reporting are available independently of entering the room. An own offer can be reported within24hours,even after blocking. Encrypted invitation evidence supports existing scoped assigned safety review but cannot establish room-ejection authority. Reports never sanction automatically. See ADR0059 for bounds and observation limits;actual fullcheck/browser/privacy results are recorded in m18-private-checks.json.

Existing shared report/block controls also appear during an actual visit. Release requires all social safety and operator gates; this local scene is not production approval. No external media, books, food photographs, audio or shipping data are included. Source provenance is served at `/assets/restaurant/provenance.json` and releaseApproved remains false.
