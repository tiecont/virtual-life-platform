# Brand, safety and scope decisions — independent mini-games

Reference inspiration: PAWS (color/action matching), Bluffalo Club (bluff/challenge), Fishy Business (cooperative hidden sabotage). Party Animals official update notes describe these categories; source links at bottom. **No code, art, marks, character models, rulebook wording, cards, item names, animations, audio, videos or screenshots from Party Animals are provided or licensed by this plan.** Do not use the three Party Animals names in the shipped game/marketing except as a properly attributed comparative reference reviewed for legal risk. Mechanics alone do not justify reproducing unique creative expression; get independent review where appropriate.

| Decision | v1 recommendation |
|---|---|
| IP/style | Original cozy Lantern Lodge with unique tiles/cards, icons, characters and audio; provenance ledger and content review before distribution |
| Bluff consequence | Credibility points; **no roulette gun or random injury/death** |
| Monetization | No wagers, gambling, paid chance rewards or cash-equivalent trading; cosmetic shop outside MVP and only after platform store review |
| Social | Existing approved quick emotes, report/block; public free chat/audio remains subject to separate platform safety gate |
| Dating | New games are not restricted 18+ Dating content; cannot route hidden-match data into Dating or infer player age/relationship |
| Hidden roles | In-game deception is not evidence of abusive real-account behavior; safety reports are separate and confidential |
| Accessibility | Glyph + readable name for color; semantic card controls; optional animation/audio; mobile touch design from the start |
| Rewards | Existing progression policy stays default OFF; no farmable XP until abuse budgets are defined |
| Data privacy | Public/admin projections aggregate-only; optional seed-fairness reveal after match only if reviewed; bounded encrypted private state |
