## Adult eligibility and access plan

Only registered accounts may enter the eventual prototype. Minimum age is18. Unknown,underage,expired/revoked/mismatched proof,missing provider,missing reviewed process or missing operational approval denies positive participation. Self-entered birthdate,an18+checkbox,client role,guest session,costume or arbitrary boolean request is insufficient. The current city has **no effective real-world age verification implementation** and does not claim that self-attestation or synthetic tests establish age.

Before any real participant pilot,the owner must select a reviewed verification process/provider,document its assurance limits,privacy,data transfer,revocation,retention and support/fraud pathways,and obtain the applicable human/legal/operational review. The application should retain only an opaque account-bound decision/issuer-policy reference,verification/expiry times and revocation status. Proposed proof age is at most24hours,also clipped by issuer expiry;the operator may require shorter. Raw age/identity documents,real names,exact birthdates or images must not be ingested into city tables/logs/analytics. No third-party account linkage or provider call is authorized by this plan.

Every HTTP/socket/Room/travel/deep-link positive path rechecks the current decision,registered session,world gates,sanctions,incident state and explicit dating/profile/match consent. A cached browser flag or former accepted proposal cannot authorize later contact. On eligibility/coverage/source loss,stop positive activity,close private communication and require fresh valid consent after recovery. Independent Leave,own consent withdrawal,block,private report/receipt and privacy export/erasure must remain available without restoring positive admission. Tests use explicitly synthetic proof ports/fixtures in disposable local environments and cannot certify legal effectiveness,identity uniqueness or evasion resistance.

## Separate data and consent

The dating owner must keep a separate minimal private profile and mutual matching preferences. A general nickname/avatar/friendship/session is not permission to disclose romantic interests or participation. Future matching inputs must be finite,bounded and private;do not require gender,exact age,location,photos,biography or other sensitive detail merely to demonstrate an algorithm. If a later product needs additional sensitive attributes,document purpose,consent,retention,export and independent review before introducing them. A neutral private alias may be explicitly chosen;do not silently copy a public profile into match views.

Profile opt-in,queue entry,two-party proposal acceptance,private quick-chat/date participation and mutual continuation are distinct choices. One account can be in at most one open proposal/date. No automatic requeue/reconnect or late retry may restore withdrawn consent. Decline/expire/Leave/block wins against delayed positive replies through durable versions/receipts and negative-consent fences. Return only opaque match/session handles and the minimum currently authorized private peer projection;generic profile lookup/global notifications must contain no dating fields. A mutual continuation is a revocable private relation,not a DM,address exchange or automatic shared friendship.

Private source proof must support reporting a matched contact without revealing a reporter to the subject or using global public-presence opt-in. Reports are encrypted allegations managed by the existing Safety owner,not automatic findings/sanctions. Scope reviewers to assigned,conflict-free cases;staff abuse and appeals require independent oversight. Block suppresses all later contact and ends pending/active dating work atomically. Keep safe Exit reachable by native keyboard/mobile controls and independent of a pending match/chat request. No user must reveal personal details to leave or report.

## Provisional lifecycle and retention

The strict draft sets a120second queue,45second proposal and300second private date,all server-authoritative and bounded. These are design defaults,not a runtime timer implementation. Proposed cleanup limits are30days for inactive profile/continuation,24hours for eligibility metadata/proposal/date history/mutation receipts,and90days for minimal negative-consent fences. Store no raw quick-chat history by default. Counters/timestamps for replay/abuse prevention must not become dating analytics. An end/Leave hides peer projections immediately;retained metadata does not grant continued access. Erasure withdraws consent/ends contact before deleting owner data and redacts peer linkage where needed.

All durations are **unreviewed draft defaults**. M20-02..05 implement separate profile/matching/date/continuation tables with bounded cleanup/export/erasure;the consolidated adversarial matrix remains M20-06. The Safety owner retains confidential evidence under its separately reviewed policy/key/hold rules rather than copying allegations into dating history. Human privacy/legal review must decide actual retention,erasure exceptions,backup effects,access/export policy and contact before pilot. Do not claim a draft number establishes a lawful retention period or that deletion from a live table erases backups.
